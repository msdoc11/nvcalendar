# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Discuss the code, not the
person writing it.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the work: what it does, what it costs, what it breaks.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A project stays usable by leaving things
  out.
- Give people room to be wrong, including yourself. A correction is not an
  accusation.
- Respect the effort behind a report or a patch, even when it cannot be merged.

## What is not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's identity, background or
  ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: repeatedly reopening settled decisions, or derailing
  threads.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space this
project uses, and to public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Email <email@qsyro.com>. Reports stay between you and the maintainers.

Include what happened, where, and links if there are any. You will get a reply
within a few days.

## Consequences

A maintainer may edit or delete a comment, close a thread, or block an account,
and will say when a decision has been made and why. Repeated or severe breaches
lead to a permanent ban.
